How good is the surrogate

What this is, and isn't

  • Trained on 8,000 simulated exposure fields (Latin-hypercube over all six knobs) with scanner dose/focus jitter and CD-SEM noise added.
  • The simulator chains Abbe partially coherent imaging, defocus and spherical aberration, a swing curve, acid generation, PEB diffusion and threshold development.
  • It is not calibrated to a real resist or scanner. Absolute numbers are illustrative; trends and workflow are the point.
  • Inference runs entirely in this page: a 3-layer neural network, about a microsecond per field.
